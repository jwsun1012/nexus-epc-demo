00:00:00,000 Higher fidelity for d electron materials

We provide higher-fidelity electron-phonon interaction data for materials where conventional P B E calculations fall short, especially compounds with d electrons. These are materials where the electronic structure and atomic vibrations can be difficult to predict. Our SCAN and r-squared SCAN workflow is built to make that data more reliable.

00:00:23,100 Published comparison with PBE

Cobalt oxide is a clear example. P B E predicts a metallic state and spurious lattice instabilities. Our r-squared SCAN calculations recover an insulating state and remove those instabilities, with phonons closer to experiment. Together with the nickel oxide benchmarks, this shows why advanced functionals can provide substantially more faithful coupling data for these d-electron systems.

00:00:49,100 Accuracy efficiency and transferability

The aim is to bring accuracy, efficiency, and transferability together. We use the same functional framework across different material classes, without fitting a Hubbard U for each material. That gives us a practical route to higher-fidelity datasets for superconductors, semiconductors, and energy materials. Researchers can trace each result back to its calculation.
