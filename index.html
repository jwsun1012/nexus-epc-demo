Predicting Electron-Phonon Interactions Tool

About this demonstration

The presentation shows precomputed published results and explains the VASP calculation workflow. It does not execute a new VASP calculation or integrate a new raw spectrum. MgB2 illustrates transferability; CoO and NiO provide the evidence for improved treatment of d-electron materials.

Published figures adapted from Y. Wang et al., “Accurate Electron-Phonon Interactions from Advanced Density Functional Theory,” PRX Energy 5, 013002 (2026). Read the paper. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. CoO and MgB2 figure panels are cropped from the originals.

Narration uses an AI-generated voice.
